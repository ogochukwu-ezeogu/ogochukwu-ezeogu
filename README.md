## 🚀 About Me

I'm a Data Analyst focused on turning raw datasets into decisions people can actually act on. I work primarily in R and SQL, building analyses that go beyond dashboards, identifying real patterns in the data, rigorously testing them, and communicating what they mean to the people who have to act on them.

I care about getting the details right: proper handling of missing data, clinically/domain-grounded thresholds instead of arbitrary cutoffs, and knowing when a correlation needs a model behind it before it's trustworthy. I believe the value of an analysis is in the decisions it enables, not the visuals it produces.

When I'm not deep in a dataset, I'm sharpening my stats fundamentals, developing health articles or exploring new tools, and chasing that moment when the data finally says something clear. 💹
